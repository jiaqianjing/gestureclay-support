隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日

GestureClay 手势飞碟不创建账户，不收集、传输、出售或共享任何个人数据。App 不包含广告、分析、追踪、第三方 SDK 或任何网络请求。

摄像头。App 使用 iPad 的前置原深感摄像头识别手势和手部动作。摄像头画面和深度数据只在设备上实时处理，用完即丢弃，不会被录制、保存或上传。

本地数据。关卡进度、星级、手势灵敏度校准和设置保存在你的设备上，只用于提供游戏功能。删除 App 即可清除这些数据。开发者选项中的数据记录功能只有在你手动开启时才会在本机保存统计数字，不会上传。

儿童。App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

如对本政策有疑问，请通过 支持页面 联系我们。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

GestureClay does not create accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs or network requests.

Camera. The app uses the iPad's front TrueDepth camera to recognize your hand gestures. Camera frames and depth data are processed on device in real time and discarded; they are never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Local data. Stage progress, stars, gesture calibration and settings are stored only on your device to provide the game. Deleting the app removes them. The developer-mode recording feature stores aggregate numbers on device only, and only when you turn it on.

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
